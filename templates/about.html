🌿 About YogaMood

Your Mood. Your Yoga. Your Balance.

YogaMood is a mood-based yoga website designed to help users discover suitable yoga practices, breathing exercises and relaxation activities.

🌱 Our Project

What is YogaMood?

YogaMood is a web-based project that connects mood selection with yoga recommendations.

Users can select how they are feeling and explore suitable yoga practices. The website also provides yoga details, video resources, breathing exercises and a relaxation timer.

User accounts, mood history and feedback are managed using a Flask backend and SQLite database.
